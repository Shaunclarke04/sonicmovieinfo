Synopsis (Spoiler Warning)

Longclaw is introduced as Sonic’s guardian/mentor and explains there is a war between the Owls and the Echidnas. Longclaw sends sonic to earth to protect him.

Sonic, During a game of baseball, gets upset and runs around in circles generating energy and causing a blackout across the entirety of Green Hill. This starts an investigation for which the government calls on Dr. Ivo Robotnik to investigate the cause of the EMP with the help of his associate, Agent Stone.

Sonic seeks refuge and is found by Tom ‘Donut Lord’ Wachowski who helps him get to San Francisco to retrieve his lost rings, recruiting the help of Maddie, Tom’s wife. 

Sonic and Tom fight Robotnik, sending him to the mushroom planet. Sonic is then brought home and shown his new room in the Wachowski house.

Synopsis (Spoiler Warning)

Sonic is left home alone with Ozzie for the first time while Tom and Maddie go to Maddie’s Sister, Rachel’s, Wedding. We later find out that the wedding was a setup by G.U.N in order to catch Sonic and Tails.

During their absence, the Wachowski house is invaded by Eggman who has brought along Knuckles the Echidna. Sonic Is overwhelmed by Knuckles until being rescued by Miles ‘Tails’ Prower, a young two-tailed fox who has been observing Sonic since the initial EMP blast.

Sonic and Tails go off on their own to find and retrieve the master emerald which Longclaw has left Sonic in charge of after her sacrifice. Little do they know that Eggman and Knuckles have teamed up to get there before them.

Eggman betrays knuckles and decides to keep the emerald for himself before knuckles joins forces with Sonic and Tails to defeat Eggman once again as Super Sonic, leaving him assumed dead.

Synopsis (Spoiler Warning)

With Shadow loose in Tokyo, G.U.N’s Director Rockwell call on Sonic, Tails and Knuckles to help capture shadow. They meet with Commander Walters who explains Shadow’s story and his connection to “Project Shadow” to the team shortly before being killed by Rouge Eggdrones stolen from Eggman, who Agent Stone reveals was not behind the attack

Before his death, Walters entrusts sonic with one of two keys to the eclipse canon held in the River Themes in central London, which G.U.N built to Gerald's design and specifications, capable of wiping out entire planets in one shot. Rockwell searches Walters for the key, unable to find it, she assumes sonic must have stolen it.

After losing trust in G.U.N, the team quickly head back home to recruit the help of Tom and Maddie - who were bored with nothing to do – they hatch a plan to break into G.U.Ns vault to steal back the second key to keep it away from the Robotniks

The Eggmen eventually do retrieve both keys and begin to fire the canon with the help of Shadow’s chaos energy to power it despite a warning from Stone that Gerald wasn’t being completely honest with Ivo

After Ivo and Gerald’s falling out, Sonic and Shadow put aside their differences to help Ivo and Tails to stop the canon and save the planet.

Synopsis (Spoiler Warning)

Currently unrealised, nothing is known about the movie as of now, however it is presumed to be a detailed history of Shadow, Maria, Gerald and the operations and roles of G.U.N 

[assuming this is the case]
In 1974, a meteorite crash landed in Oklahoma containing an alien hedgehog named Shadow and is seized by the US military. A new Agency, G.U.N (Guardian Units of Nations), under supervision of Commander Walters was formed and Professor Gerald Robotnik was tasked with researching Shadow’s Powers.

Gerald’s Granddaughter, Maria develops a close friendship with Shadow; one night, after an escape attempt by Gerald, Maria and Shadow, Maria is caught in crossfire while guards are targeting shadow despite pleads from Walters to hold fire.

Shadow is then held in containment thereafter, until he is mysteriously released and makes his way to Tokyo, Japan which follows into the events of the Third Movie.
