Welcome to Sonic Movie Info!

This is not a news source nor is it an official source endorsed by Paramount or Sega. It's a fan project designed to provide a reference to important canon, events, and other details about the franchise

It is not designed to replace the SCU Wiki, and I recommend checking there for more detailed information about the franchise and its characters. This site is simply an overview of the most important aspects in an easily browsable format for quick reference.

We have

  • A list of every movie and show in release order, each with a list of characters introduced in that entry and a detailed synopsis
  • Family trees for the Robotniks and Wachowskis
  • A comprehensive list of characters in the franchise with detailed biographies
  • A, scrollable, interactive timeline of canon events
